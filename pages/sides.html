Mexican (Brown) Rice

Here is a recipe for Mexican Rice made from brown rice.

Ingredients

Pantry/Dry Food
  • 1 Cup of Brown Rice
  • 2 Tbsp of Oil of choice (vegetable, canola, olive, etc.)
  • 1/2 Tsp of Tomato Paste
Seasonings
  • 1.5 Tbsp of Chicken Bouillon
  • 1 Tsp of Tomato Bouillon
  • 1/4 Tsp of Cumin
  • 1/4 Tsp of Onion Powder
  • 1/4 Tsp of Garlic Powder
  • 1/4 Tsp of Dried Oregano
  • 1/8 Tsp of Salt
  • 1/8 Tsp of Pepper
  • Optional: 1/4 Tsp of Dried Cilantro
Other
  • 3.75 Cups of Water

Equipment

Appliances
  • Stovetop
Standard Tools
  • Sauteeing Pan w/ Lid
  • Spatula
  • Measuring Cup
  • Measuring Spoons
  • Mesh Strainer or Bowl (to rinse rice)

Instructions

  1. Rinse/Clean your rice thoroughly.
  2. Preheat 2 Tbsp of oil in the pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add your rice to the pan and saute until fragrant and golden-brown.
  4. Add your tomato paste and stir to combine.
  5. Add your water and all seasonings.
  6. Bring to a boil, reduce heat to low, then cover with a lid and simmer for 55 minutes.
  7. Remove from heat and let sit for 5-10 minutes before serving.
  8. Congratulate yourself and be fulfilled!
